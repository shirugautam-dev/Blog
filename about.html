About

Srijana

Hi, I’m Srijana!

I started this blog out of my love for reading and writing. It’s a space where I share my honest thoughts, reflections, book reviews, and little pieces of life.

I like writing about the things that make me stop and think — books I have read, experiences I have had, ideas I cannot quite let go of, and the ordinary moments that sometimes turn out to be worth remembering.

I don’t always have an answer. Sometimes I am simply trying to understand something by writing about it.

Thank you for being here. I hope you find something here that resonates with you.
