# Carl Jung’s Synchronicity Gave Me an Eerie Feeling

<h2 class="article-subtitle">Three coincidences in two weeks. Mathematics, psychology and Carl Jung. All fail to convince me!</h2>

![An elderly man wearing glasses peeks out from behind a white board, covering his mouth in surprise, symbolising the unexpected and eerie nature of coincidences.](../Images/articles/synchronicity.jpg "Photo by krakenimages on Unsplash")

## Three coincidences
At last, I caved in and watched Breaking Bad. I had watched a couple of episodes sometime back, but thinking it was about drugs, not a topic that I enjoy, I didn’t follow through. But last month, after watching series of K-Drama and J-Drama and Irish drama, I needed change. I needed a drama of a different kind.

Netflix got into what it does best: suggesting shows that I can binge-watch. By now, it knows I am good at it. Breaking Bad was the first on the list. It won. I got convinced that I needed to watch it. After all, a 9.5 IMDB rating means something. I might be missing something really good. And to Netflix’s satisfaction, I binged-watched it. In twenty days, five seasons, 62 episodes!

I enjoyed it immensely, and by the time I finished, I was exhausted. I needed a break from Netflix. I vowed I would not turn on the TV for three days. During that time, I kept on thinking about the show. How would I not? After all the time I had spent on it. Again and again, I thought about Walter Jr., played by RJ Mitte. What is he doing now? Does he really have cerebral palsy? But I didn’t Google him, surprisingly. Most of the time I do.

Then, after three days, he was on the news. He committed suicide. I was taken aback. I was sad, of course, but also stunned by the fact that it happened now, when I had just finished watching the show. How strange is that?

That is not all.

When I was in Netflix detox phase of three days, I started reading You With The Sad Eyes by Christina Applegate. I was in the middle of it when she announced in her podcast MeSsy that she was misdiagnosed with Multiple Sclerosis. She said she is back to square one, not knowing what disease she is suffering from. It was sad and heartbreaking to listen to her. Then again, I am struck by the timing. It could have happened a month back, when I had no clue about the book or a month later, when I would have moved on. But it happened when I was exactly reading it.

[What were the chances](luck-fate.html)? Odd, isn’t it?

What really made me write this article is yet another coincidence. Three nights back, I dreamt about a friend I have not spoken to for eight years. When I woke up in the morning, it was a nice feeling. I thought I needed to talk to him. It’s been too long.

That very evening, his mother passed away.

This was too many coincidences in two weeks. I am zapped. I kind of feel eerie. I want to know how this happens. Is it even possible?

## The Law of Truly Large Numbers
Mathematically, these kinds of coincidences are possible, according to David Hand, a professor of mathematics. He argues that extreme coincidences are mathematically inevitable. If there are enough time, people and opportunities, the rare events must happen. It’s called the Law of Truly Large Numbers. What seems impossible is not really impossible.

## Apophenia and selective attention
Modern psychology calls it apophenia- the tendency to detect patterns in unrelated or random information. There is another theory of selective attention which says that we are more likely to notice the events when we are already thinking about them.

## Carl Jung’s synchronicity
Carl Jung called it synchronicity, which he defined as an “acausal connecting principle.” Two events may happen at the same time without having a direct cause-and-effect relationship between them, but share a deep and symbolic connection.

He believed that’s how the universe operates. We are not isolated minds but a collective unconscious. We are all related on the psychic level. [The human mind and the outside world are not separate](death.html). When the boundary between these two dissolves momentarily, synchronicity happens.

If Jung were here today and judged my incidences, he would probably have said that my psychic energy and the events of the outside world fell perfectly into rhythm.

## But I am still confused
None of the above theories resolves my curiosity.

I am not convinced that it has got something to do with the psyche and that I am in some way feeling the vibes of the universe.

Psychology is only telling me how I noticed the news. It is not able to explain the timing part.

Chances were so slim; it’s difficult to settle with mathematical probability also.

I am still at the same point where I started. My mind is still searching for the answers.
