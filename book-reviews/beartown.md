Once I read The Answer Is No by Fredrik Backman, there was no going back. I started reading his novels one after the other. Among the ones I have read, this is my favourite. Unlike The Answer Is No, this one kept me busy for a few days. And after I finished it, I was not able to start another book for a week; I wanted to stay with the feeling for some time. It felt like cheating if I did.

I started this thinking it would be an underdog story, small-town teenagers going against all odds to win a hockey tournament. I expected a good read because it was written by Backman, but not in my wildest dream I thought it was going to be an emotional rollercoaster.

It is Part 1 of the Beartown Trilogy. The story is set in a small fictional town called Beartown, where hockey is like a religion. They don’t have much going on; the town is dying slowly, and if they can win a tournament, the town would get some visibility, and the businesses can come back. The whole town prepares for it. But a violent crime happens outside of the game, and each one in the town is tested, and each one takes a side.

<em>“Everyone has a thousand wishes before a tragedy, but just one afterwards.”</em>

The strength of this novel lies in the characters. Backman builds them such that we can see ourselves in them. Normal people trying to live their lives in the best way possible. Like us, each one of them is complicated and flawed. Each one of them has their own story. And when the time comes, they want to protect the one who has more value to the town, not the one who really needs protection, like how our society behaves, and the story becomes uncomfortably real.

Every word is convincing, every scene is believable. It made me lose myself; I could read it for hours and did not want it to end. I started thinking in the middle of it: what would I read after that would match this?

It is an absolute read if you are a Backman fan. And if you have not read him, it’s time you start.
