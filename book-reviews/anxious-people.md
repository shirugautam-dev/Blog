    Let me start by saying I'm a Fredrik Backman fan. 

    This is his fourth book that I have read. Somehow, this time around, it failed to give me the pleasure the other three had given.

    It could not hook me immediately. The story came alive after a few chapters, a bit too long a waiting time for me. If it were not by Backman, I would have given up already. Since I trusted the author, I continued. It got more interesting in the second half, each chapter revealing something new. 

    What bothered me the most was that I could not really connect to any of the characters. They even annoyed me sometimes. 

    A bank robber who is on the loose after trying to rob a cashless bank enters an apartment across the street. The apartment is for sale, and a few prospective buyers are already there. The buyers who are hostages now are the worst ones at that. They are neither afraid of the robber nor sad about the situation they are in. 

    On the other side, there are policemen trying to figure out who the robber is and how to catch him. Will they catch him? 	 
  
    As the author always does, in this one also, he tries to explore humans. The story touches on topics like suicide, loneliness, grief, cancer, anxiety, addiction and more. 

    <em>“Addicts are addicted to their drugs, and their families are addicted to hope.”</em>

    Most of the chapters are dialogue only, interviews of the hostages by the policemen. This is another thing that bothered me. None of the hostages answered any of the questions from the policemen. They were all obnoxious to the point that it was unbelievable.  

    I felt that the author tried a bit too hard to be witty and funny.   

    Many people have enjoyed this novel, and it has been made into a TV series as well. So, all I can say is that this one was not for me.
