    This was again something I picked up because it was short and was in Kindle Unlimited. I have to say it was a delight. 

    What is different about this is that the whole story is told through letters. The author grabbed me right away, and I read it in one go. The characters are believable, the words are smooth, and the ending gave me a satisfying smile.

    Carrie Allsop and David Mayer find that their spouses are having an affair. Not knowing what exactly they can do, they start looking for evidence and write letters to each other. In the beginning, they are unsure of this, but as time passes, they themselves form a deep connection.

    The author is successful in portraying how it feels to be cheated, the confusion that follows and how one can still find strength even when the heart is broken. It also shows how we find solace in strangers when our own deceive us. 

    <em>“It is funny the crazy things our brains make up to save us from the truth.”</em>

    I have already bought Atmosphere by the author and expect her to delight me the same.
