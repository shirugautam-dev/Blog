This is my first book by Taylor Jenkins Reid. I was looking for something to read, not having any particular book in mind. In Kindle Unlimited, it was free and very short, only 88 pages. It was perfect for that Sunday when I had nothing else to do. I have to say it’s a delight.

What grabbed me immediately was the way it’s written, which is quite unlike others. The whole story is told through letters. There are two main characters who write letters to each other, and also two other characters whose letters these main characters share with each other. The plot moves through these letters only.

The characters are so believable that I sympathised with them right away. The words are so smooth that I got immersed in it, never wanting to put it down. And the ending is so satisfying that I had a smile on my face when I finished.

Carrie Allsop suspects that her husband is cheating on her with David Mayer’s wife. She writes him a letter wondering if he knew about it. David, not having a clue at that time, agrees to find evidence of the affair on his side too. They both then start looking at their spouses’ stuff and writing letters to each other about their findings. In the beginning, they are unsure of what they are doing. They are both heartbroken and trying to figure out what they should do now. But as time passes by, they themselves form a deep connection.

In this novella, we can see how [human relationships](the-answer-is-no.html) are developed. When in despair, how we want to find answers and look for support from strangers who become our confidantes. Someone close becomes a stranger, and some stranger becomes close in no time when the heart is broken.

The author is successful in portraying how it feels to be cheated, the confusion that follows and how one can still find strength even when the world seems to be upside down. We find solace in strangers when our own deceive us.

<em>“It is funny the crazy things our brains make up to save us from the truth.”</em>

I am happy I found the author accidentally.

I have already bought [Atmosphere](atmosphere.html) by her and expect her to delight me the same.
